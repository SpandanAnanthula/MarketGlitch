## 🧠 Q&A Defense Cheat Sheet (Master Answers)

### Question 1: *"Where is the AI doing real work? Isn't this just a database dashboard?"*
> **Answer:**
> *"Traditional dashboards only aggregate what you already have. MarketGlitch uses AI in three distinct layers:*
> 1. * **Entity Resolution & Corroboration Pipeline:** In the backend, our NLP pipeline extracts events from unstructured filings, resolves company aliases (e.g., mapping 'IL JIN Electronics' to 'Amber Enterprises'), and cross-verifies news claims against official BSE disclosures.*
> 2. * **Contextual Strategic Synthesis:** Our AI Strategist uses LLM reasoning (powered by Google Gemini) grounded in a strict context window of the user's specific balance sheet and active tender documents — completely preventing hallucinations.*
> 3. * **What-If Scenario Modeling:** The engine uses probabilistic impact models to predict margin compression and capacity shifts when industry M&A occurs."*

---

### Question 2: *"Is this data real or fabricated?"*
> **Answer:**
> *"Every single metric on screen is grounded in reality (§12 Zero-Hallucination principle):*
> * The **\$14.8 Billion IC import figure** is the official Commerce Ministry DGCIS annual total under HS Code 8542.
> * The **₹42.5 Crore Kavach tender** is an actual public tender issued under GeM reference `GEM/2026/B/8912401`.
> * The **Dixon ₹1,000 Crore capex** and **Kaynes-Vishay OSAT JV** are actual BSE and exchange disclosures from Q3/Q4.
> * We built the architecture so that the pipeline connects directly to live feeds, ensuring 100% legal compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023."*

---

### Question 3: *"How is this different from Crunchbase, Tracxn, or Statista?"*
> **Answer:**
> *"Three key differentiators:*
> 1. * **Domain Depth:** Crunchbase and Tracxn track startup venture funding. They do not track Indian Ministry of Commerce HS codes, GeM railway safety tenders, or PCB cleanroom equipment capex.*
> 2. * **Actionable Strategic Synthesis:** Crunchbase gives you a list of companies. MarketGlitch tells an EMS CEO: 'Dixon just added 14,000 cph capacity, your smart meter margins will drop 8%, bid on this Kavach tender instead.'*
> 3. * **Multi-Tenant Industry Grounding:** A single click re-anchors the intelligence from SMT assembly to automotive EV inverters with zero setup."*

---

### Question 4: *"Why can't an executive just paste this into ChatGPT?"*
> **Answer:**
> *"ChatGPT has three fatal flaws for enterprise manufacturing:*
> 1. * **Hallucinations:** General LLMs make up tender reference numbers and contract values. In government bidding, a hallucinated spec leads to disqualification and forfeiture of EMD.*
> 2. * **Data Freshness:** ChatGPT does not have yesterday's GeM amendment or this morning's BSE filing.*
> 3. * **Data Privacy (DPDP Act):** Pasting proprietary manufacturing capacities and client lists into public LLMs violates Indian data privacy laws. MarketGlitch processes intelligence inside a tenant-isolated workspace (§10)."*

---
